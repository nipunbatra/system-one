How an LLM writes an answer, and how decision models such as Jev skip the writing and return calibrated probabilities in one forward pass. About 60 to 80 minutes with the notebook demos.

What the lecture covers

  1. Two ways of thinking. Fast judgements and slow reasoning, in people and in software.
  2. How an LLM answers. Tokens, attention, logits, softmax and the generation loop.
  3. Read, don't write. Taking option probabilities from one forward pass.
  4. Calibration. Reliability diagrams, ECE by hand and in Python, temperature scaling.
  5. Training for calibration. RLHF, RLVR and RLCD; Brier and log score; a trained decision head.
  6. Putting it to work. Thresholds, pipelines with an LLM, and the limits reported so far.
