Draw, don't write

Can a model that only answers questions make a picture? Here an off-the-shelf language model, with no training, draws a 500 × 500 picture with one channel (grey). It answers “dark or bright?” once per cell of a grid, 25, 100 or 784 times, and the page blends the pixels between.

Nothing was trainedEvery pixel comes from two-way choices read off a chat model, then arithmetic. No image model is involved.

    
Do more questions help?Try it: 100 and 784 questions are in the menu. The model has to know which place you mean. It follows words such as “top left”, but it barely follows “36% from the left”, so the 28 × 28 picture is mostly noise.

    
Why it is blurryThe useful answers are the 25 named ones. The last step only blends between cells, so nothing finer than a region can appear. Full size, one question per pixel, would be 250,000 passes.

    
What training buysA trained model picks 128 picture codes out of 8,192 in one pass, and a trained decoder turns the codes into sharp pixels (Liu et al., arXiv 2611.24738). Same building block, a choice, but learned.
